# SRB2Kart Saturn note:
Due to a good amount of issues with newer SDL releases, we decided to ship our own Version of the library.
This version is just the 2.28.5 release with a fix backported for non US keyboard layouts that prevented the right ALT GR key from working properly.
It can be found under: https://github.com/alufolie91/SDL/tree/backportaltgrfix
